Общий промпт: `Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: individual product image for a bicycle shop desktop application. Primary request: professional realistic studio catalog photograph of [subject]. One bicycle only. Entire bicycle visible in perfect side profile, wheels both completely inside frame. Wide landscape image, subject centered with generous 12 percent padding, pale warm off-white seamless backdrop, floor with subtle contact shadow. Clean premium catalog photography, believable mechanical details, realistic chain and frame, soft daylight. No people, no logos, no letters, no text, no watermark, no decorative graphic elements. This is a generic demo product, not a real brand.`

Subjects:

- city: `A sage green step-through city bicycle, black saddle, swept handlebars, slim tires, rear rack, tan sidewall tires`.
- trail: `A burnt orange hardtail mountain bicycle, front suspension, chunky black 29-inch tires, flat black handlebars, black saddle`.
- road: `A midnight navy road bicycle, drop handlebars, thin black tires, aerodynamic frame, black saddle`.
- junior: `A golden yellow children's bicycle, 20 inch wheels, compact diamond frame, flat handlebars, black seat, no training wheels`.

`brand.svg` — новый векторный знак, нарисованный кодом. Все ресурсы подключены через Go embed.
